Interpretation

  • screening means thresholds were not user-confirmed; it is not a pass or fail.
  • not_assessable is a documented evidence gap, not evidence of adequacy.
  • Candidate discovery and source labels never prove inclusion, recall, saturation, or screening traceability.

Limitations

  • This report audits the supplied, scope-filtered record set. It cannot establish that global literature has been found.
  • Missing year or language metadata is excluded whenever that boundary is declared, rather than silently included.
